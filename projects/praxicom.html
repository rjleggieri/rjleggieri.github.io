Archived academic prototype · Spring 2025 · UT Austin NURSING-AI Challenge

PraxiCom: a faculty-directed voice simulation

A synthetic patient conversation prototype developed individually with guidance from UT Austin nursing faculty.

Public case study ↗Watch demo ↗

What I built

I built the React student and faculty interfaces, Node.js and Socket.IO session relay, YAML patient personas, and faculty evaluation workflow. Hume EVI supplied the streaming voice layer. GuideLLM-informed prompting organized goals and conversational context.

Design choices and limits

The prototype favored rapid voice integration and faculty visibility. A modular speech pipeline was identified as a possible next step for finer prompt control. Only the Advanced Directives scenario was configured for live voice in the archived prototype.

This was a proof of concept, without authentication, durable storage, or formal privacy controls. Included patients are synthetic; the prototype is not a validated clinical product or educational intervention and should not handle real patient or student records.

What carries forward

Working with domain experts, turning goals into interface and session requirements, integrating external AI services, and making an interaction inspectable by a human reviewer.
