Additional graduate coursework

Supporting methods and implementation experience from private course repositories. These summaries do not distribute restricted starter code or assignment materials.

Model adaptation and representation

  • Implemented mixed precision, LoRA, four-bit quantization, and QLoRA.
  • Built patch autoencoders, Binary Spherical Quantization, and autoregressive image models.
  • Implemented structured prompting, custom generation, and parameter-efficient adaptation for mathematical reasoning in smaller language models.

Synthetic data and simulated planning

Built a SuperTuxKart pipeline producing nearly one million grounded vision-language question-answer pairs. Implemented an MLP using lane boundaries, a Perceiver-inspired transformer with learned queries, and an image-to-waypoint CNN. These are simulated coursework implementations, rather than real-world ADAS development.

Foundations studied

Convex and stochastic optimization, online learning and bandits, reinforcement learning, symbolic and continuous planning, Bayesian state estimation, MDPs and POMDPs, and ethical approaches to fairness, accountability, and deployment.
