Graduate NLP project · UT Austin

Evaluating NLP robustness through training-data analysis

I instrumented DeBERTa training, mapped example-level learning dynamics, and built a category-weighted loss to test how the choice of training signal affects generalization.

  • Custom Hugging Face training callback and Trainer override
  • Weighted PyTorch dataset and cross-entropy loss
  • Standard validation alongside adversarial subcase evaluation
HANS accuracy77.49 → 78.59%+1.10 percentage points
MNLI accuracy88.91 → 88.47%−0.44 percentage points

A modest challenge-set gain, with an in-domain trade-off and persistent failures in difficult constructions.

05 / Exploratory graduate research · UT Austin

When text and voice tell different stories.

I designed and evaluated an Alignment Discrepancy Score (ADS) across 189 DAIC-WOZ interviews, with utterance alignment and five-fold participant-level cross-validation.

0.62 ADS AUC0.49 audio-only0.70 text-only
PythonSpeech & NLPMultimodal evaluation

The discrepancy score trailed the text-only baseline. The result motivated closer work on measurement, calibration, and evidence boundaries; it is not a diagnostic tool.
