04 / Multimodal research · UT Austin · Public Python framework

Text–prosody alignment: from research question to working pipeline.

I designed the Alignment Discrepancy Score (ADS) and built an end-to-end text/audio pipeline across 189 DAIC-WOZ interviews: participant utterance extraction, timestamped audio clips, pretrained affect models, session features, and participant-level evaluation.

Sentiment-based ADS produced stronger discrimination than the emotion-based variant. Further precision–recall analysis reached AUPRC 0.410 against a 0.302 prevalence baseline. Distribution and threshold analyses added an interpretable view of where the signal appeared and what trade-offs it involved.

I extended the research into text-prosody-evidence: reusable software for grouping, nested evaluation, calibration, reliability, and provenance. Its public text-component benchmark improved valence RMSE from 0.4276 to 0.3908 using source-document-grouped evaluation.
