Graduate NLP project · UT Austin

Evaluating NLP robustness through training-data analysis

Instrumented DeBERTa training across 392,702 MNLI examples, implemented category-weighted loss, and compared standard validation with the HANS challenge set.

Reported HANS accuracy: 77.49% → 78.59%
MNLI accuracy: 88.91% → 88.47%. A modest challenge-set gain with an in-domain trade-off.
